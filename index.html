You have one simple mission: get Chad's science homework from your house to his. Sounds easy. But the moment you step outside, anxiety starts doing what anxiety does.

Start The Walk

World Overview

Every walk to Chad's is its own experience. People nearby raise your anxiety just by existing. Sometimes it spikes for no reason at all. Building walls give you a moment to breathe and reset.

How To Play

Move

WASD to move through the neighborhood.

Hoodie

I toggles hoodie up/down. It helps. Somehow it always helps.

Phone

O toggles phone out/away. Something to look at. Takes the edge off, costs you speed.

Wall-Flower

P near a wall to stop, lean back, and decompress.

Win

Reach Chad's house with anxiety below max.

Lose

If anxiety reaches 100, you are overwhelmed and must try again.

Internal Burst

Sometimes anxiety spikes from within. Tools still help reduce the hit, but timing can still hurt.
